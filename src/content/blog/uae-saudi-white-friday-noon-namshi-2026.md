During **White Friday** and **Yellow Friday** across the UAE and Saudi Arabia, online platforms like **Noon**, **Namshi**, **Babyshop**, and **Mumzworld** slash prices by 30% to 70% — and partner bank cards add another instant 10% to 20% at checkout.

## 1. Check Bank BIN Discounts Before Checkout

Banks such as **Emirates NBD**, **ADCB**, **Mashreq**, **Al Rajhi**, and **SNB** sponsor dedicated checkout promo codes during White Friday week. Always check the minimum spend threshold and maximum discount cap per card.

## 2. Shop Kids, Beauty & Electronics Smartly

- **Baby & Kids Gear:** **Babyshop**, **Mumzworld**, and **Mamas & Papas** frequently offer bundle deals on strollers, car seats, and newborn essentials.
- **Beauty & Perfumes:** **Sephora UAE** and **Faces** run tiered gift sets alongside app-only discount codes.
