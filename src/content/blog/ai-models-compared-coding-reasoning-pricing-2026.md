Choosing between **Google Gemini**, **Anthropic Claude**, and **OpenAI ChatGPT** no longer comes down to a single benchmark — each model family dominates different daily workflows, and knowing their real strengths saves both time and subscription fees.

## 1. Coding, Refactoring & Architecture

- **Claude (Sonnet & Opus Series):** Widely favored for multi-file codebase editing, clean TypeScript/Python architecture, and following strict repository instructions inside tools like **Cursor** and **Windsurf**.
- **Weakness to Watch:** Heavy Pro usage can hit rate limits quickly during long debugging loops unless you pair it with fast autocomplete tiers.

## 2. Multimodal Understanding & Massive Context

- **Gemini (Flash & Pro Series):** Leads in native video, audio, PDF, and massive repository/document context windows, plus generous free-tier access via Google AI Studio and Workspace integrations.
- **Best Use Case:** Analyzing hour-long videos, large PDF manuals, and fast real-time agentic workflows.

## 3. General Reasoning, Voice & Everyday Productivity

- **ChatGPT (GPT & o-series):** Strong all-rounder for structured reasoning, voice mode, data analysis, and broad third-party plugin ecosystems.

## How to Test All Three Without Overpaying

Start with free developer/web tiers on **Gemini**, **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, and **Perplexity AI**, or claim **Windsurf** and **Cursor** free trials listed on BeeDND before committing to a \$20/month seat.
