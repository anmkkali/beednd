## Timing Matters More Than Promo Codes

Most retail categories follow predictable inventory cycles. Knowing when labels clear seasonal racks helps you save 30% to 50% before applying any coupon code.

### Clothing & Apparel

- **Mid-Season Markdowns:** Expect 20% to 35% off ready-to-wear and menswear lines midway through spring and autumn.
- **End-of-Season Clearance:** Unstitched fabrics, winter layers, and summer pret hit flat 40% to 50% markdowns at season close.

### Software & Hosting

- **Annual Billing + New-User Credits:** Platforms such as cloud hosts and charting tools reserve their deepest discounts for annual plans paired with verified promo codes.
