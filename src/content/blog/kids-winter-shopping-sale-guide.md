Children outgrow winter coats, sweaters, and boots faster than almost any other wardrobe category. Paying full retail price in October often means spending twice as much as necessary.

## When Kids Apparel Brands Drop Prices

Dedicated kids brands like **Minnie Minors** and **Hopscotch**, along with family retailers like **Outfitters**, **Breakout**, and **Nike**, typically run two major markdown waves:

- **Late November (Blessed Friday / Black Friday):** Flat 20% to 50% off early winter arrivals, fleece hoodies, and everyday wear.
- **Mid-to-Late Winter Clearance:** Deeper 50%+ markdowns on remaining seasonal sizes—an ideal moment to size up for next winter.

## Practical Tips for Parents

- **Filter by Kids' Categories:** Instead of scrolling through general fashion feeds, use the Kids' filter to spot dedicated children's sales quickly.
- **Size Up on Outerwear:** Buying one size up on puffer jackets and rain boots during a 50% sale stretches value across two seasons.
- **Check Exchange Policies:** Sale items frequently have shorter exchange windows, so verify chest and shoe measurements before ordering online.
