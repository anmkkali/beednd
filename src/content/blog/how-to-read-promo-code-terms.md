## Check Four Things First

A headline discount is only useful if the conditions fit your purchase. Before you copy a code, read these four items on the brand's own page.

### 1. Who the offer is for

Many offers are limited to new customers, students or members. If you already have an account, the code may be rejected.

### 2. What it covers

Look for excluded items, minimum spends and plan restrictions. Software discounts often apply only to annual billing or to the first month.

### 3. When it ends

Sale periods change without notice. If a deal has a stated end date, check it against today's date.

### 4. What happens after

For subscriptions, find the renewal price. A discount on the first period can be followed by the standard price.

## Always Confirm on the Official Site

Brands update terms often. Use this site to find the offer, then confirm the final price at checkout.
