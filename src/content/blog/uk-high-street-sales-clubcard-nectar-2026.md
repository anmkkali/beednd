UK high-street retailers run some of their sharpest markdowns between late November and **Boxing Day**. Whether you are shopping winter coats at **Next** and **Marks & Spencer**, school shoes at **Clarks** and **Start-Rite**, or skincare at **Boots**, timing your basket matters.

## 1. Time Your High-Street & Online Clearance

Most UK fashion and footwear brands release early VIP previews on their mobile apps 24 hours before public clearance goes live:

- **Next & Mini Boden:** Early-morning stock refreshes often restore popular kids' and adult winter sizes at 40% to 50% off.
- **Clarks & Start-Rite Shoes:** Look for multi-buy footwear promos and seasonal outlet drops on kids' school shoes and winter boots.

## 2. Stack Loyalty Points & UK Bank Rewards

Pair **Boots Advantage Card**, **Tesco Clubcard**, or **Nectar** points boosts with UK cashback debit/credit offers (such as **Monzo**, **Chase UK**, or **Amex UK**) to stretch every pound further during peak seasonal sales.
