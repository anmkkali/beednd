Most US shoppers leave an extra 10% to 20% on the table during **Black Friday** and **Cyber Monday** because they stop at the first advertised markdown. Combining storewide promos with loyalty tiers and US credit card portals turns a standard 30% discount into nearly half off.

## 1. Stack Store Markdowns with US Loyalty Tiers

Major US retailers like **Target**, **Best Buy**, **Walmart**, and **Nike** release early member-only price drops before opening them to the public. Joining free loyalty programs (Target Circle, My Best Buy, Nike Membership) unlocks extra app-only promo codes on top of clearance pricing.

- **Price Match Windows:** Best Buy and Target honor holiday price-match guarantees through December — keep digital receipts handy if a laptop or TV drops further on Cyber Monday.
- **Kids & Apparel Bundles:** Brands like **Carter's**, **OshKosh B'gosh**, **Old Navy**, and **Gap** routinely run stackable sitewide codes on top of doorbuster prices.

## 2. Pair with US Credit Card Cashback Portals

Before checking out online, activate merchant offers inside **Chase Offers**, **Amex Offers**, or **Capital One Shopping**. Combining a 5%–10% statement credit with a store promo code keeps your out-of-pocket total at its lowest possible price.
